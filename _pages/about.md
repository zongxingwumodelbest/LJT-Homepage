## Academic Background

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where he is working in the **HKUST NLP Group** under the supervision of Professor Junxian He. He completed his B.Eng. degree at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science** — Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024 – Present
- **B.Eng.** — Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020 – 2024

His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with specific interests in:

- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
